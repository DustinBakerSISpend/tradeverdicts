# NBA canonical transaction-date and routing policy

## Canonical date

Use the official league or team completion/approval date whenever an official source exists.

A report date, agreement-in-principle date, or draft-night intent date may be retained as source metadata, but it does not replace the canonical completion date.

For draft-rights transactions completed after draft night, use the official completion announcement date.

## Multi-team routing

When an official NBA transaction tracker or the participating teams describe one multi-team trade, model it as one canonical transaction with multiple team perspectives.

Do not split a routing leg into a separate canonical trade merely because one team-oriented raw source lists only its own assets.

## Safety boundary

A resolved audit may generate canonical candidates. It does not authorize an import, publication, indexing, advertisements, route creation, push, preview deployment, or production deployment.
